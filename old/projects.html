Fear, Volatility, and Market Segmentation: Evidence of Asymmetric Spillovers in Indian Equity Markets Using Hybrid CNN–LSTM Models

Rajib Bhattacharya · Kaushik Ghosh · Srijani Das

The study examines how market fear, represented by India VIX, influences volatility across different segments of the Indian equity market. Using daily data from 2020–2025 and a hybrid CNN–LSTM framework, the study finds that India VIX can anticipate future market volatility, with mid-cap and small-cap stocks showing stronger and more persistent reactions to fear-driven shocks.

Srijani Das · Madhurima Paul

This study examines the factors that shape student performance and well-being in India, considering living arrangements, dietary habits, self-care routines, commuting behaviors, and entertainment consumption.
